# Podcast guesting pitch template

For pitching Taylor as a guest on parenting/lifestyle podcasts. Sent to podcast hosts or producers — NOT brands.

## Structure

```
Subject: [Specific episode reference + topic angle]

[Opening: reference a specific recent episode of their show]

[Bridge: why Taylor + their audience]

[3 short episode angles, bullet form]

[Logistics: availability + a sample question or two]

[Sign-off]
```

## Length

120-200 words.

## Example

> Subject: Episode idea — why "soft girl" names are exploding right now
>
> Caught your episode with [host's recent guest] on millennial parenting paradoxes — the bit about identity-driven choices stuck with me.
>
> I'm a professional baby namer (Vogue, NYT, Today). What I'm seeing in consults right now would make a strong episode for your audience. Three directions:
>
> - Why "soft girl" names are spiking with the same parents picking maximalist nurseries
> - The science of name regret (and why it's almost always projection, not the name)
> - How TikTok rewired the naming process for Gen Z parents
>
> I'm based in [city], available for remote recordings, and I batch interviews on Tuesdays/Thursdays.
>
> Sample question if it helps the pitch: "What does the way someone names their baby say about how they think they'll parent?"
>
> Taylor

## Things to enforce

- MUST reference a specific recent episode
- Three angles, not one — gives them optionality
- Sample question is high-impact, include it
