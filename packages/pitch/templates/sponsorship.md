# Sponsorship pitch template

For paid IG/TikTok content deals.

## Structure

```
Subject: [Specific, signal-anchored — under 60 chars]

[Opening: signal hook — what specific thing about THIS brand prompted this email]

[Bridge: why Taylor + this brand makes sense, tied to her actual credibility]

[Content idea: ONE specific creative concept (not a menu of options)]

[Ask: specific next step, not "let me know"]

[warm closing sentence]

Taylor Humphrey
What's In A Baby Name
[site link]
[Cal.com link if appropriate]

[CAN-SPAM footer block from compliance gate]
```

## Tone

- Confident, expert, warm
- Specific over abstract
- One earned witty moment max
- No "hope this finds you well", "love your brand", "huge fan"

## Length

100-180 words including signature, excluding footer.

## Example (using real signal + real Taylor credibility)

> Subject: Quick partnership idea around the new Loop launch
>
> Caught the Loop launch this morning — the way you positioned "sleep is identity" lines up directly with what I'm seeing in consults this year. Parents are choosing names that mean "peaceful" or "calm" at noticeably higher rates than last year.
>
> Quick context on me in case it's helpful: I'm a professional baby name consultant — clients pay up to $30K for the concierge tier. The New Yorker did a profile that went semi-viral in 2022, and the SF Chronicle and NY Post ran follow-ups last fall. My audience on IG and TikTok skews exactly toward your launch demographic.
>
> Here's the idea: a 3-reel series where I share names that mean "sleep" or "calm" alongside the Loop bassinet — first reel in launch week, last reel tied to a giveaway.
>
> Open to a 15-min call next week to walk through it? Either way, love what you're building with Loop.
>
> Taylor Humphrey
> What's In A Baby Name
> https://www.whatsinababyname.com
> https://cal.com/taylorhumphrey/intro

## Things to enforce

- The opening MUST reference a specific, current signal
- Audience numbers MUST come from `taylor_credibility.json` — never invented
- Press references MUST be real
- The content idea MUST be specific (a series concept, a post idea, a specific tie-in), not "could collaborate"
- The ask MUST be a single concrete next step
